01 / About me

A journey inward, a practice of sharing outward.

RYT 200 journey ↗

My yoga journey began after college, practising alongside my parents. What started as a simple way to stay active gradually became something far more meaningful — a journey of self-discovery, connection, and inner growth.

Through consistent practice, I came to understand that yoga is more than movement or flexibility. It is a way of reconnecting with ourselves, cultivating awareness, and finding balance between the mind and body.

Driven by a desire to share this practice with others, I found my way to Yoga House, where I met my mentor, Sucharita. Under her guidance, I completed my 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training (RYT 200). Her wisdom, kindness, and unconditional love deeply shaped not only my practice, but also the person I am today.

The love and light she shared with me continue to inspire my journey. Now, I hope to pass that same warmth on to others — creating a space where everyone feels welcome to slow down, reconnect, and simply be themselves.

06 / Good to know

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do I need to be flexible to start?+

Not at all! Yoga is a practice to help you build flexibility and strength over time. Wherever your body is today is the perfect place to start.

Is the class beginner-friendly?+

Yes, absolutely. Every class is designed with beginners in mind. I will always offer options and modifications so you can practice at your own comfortable pace.

What should I bring?+

Please wear comfortable, stretchy clothing, and bring a water bottle. If you have your own yoga mat, please bring it along! (Note: If you don't have a mat, please let me know in advance.)

What is your cancellation policy?+

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please let me know at least 24 hours before our session. Cancellations made within 24 hours will incur the full class fee. Thank you for respecting my time!

How do I book and pay?+

Simply fill out this Google Form! Once submitted, I will reach out to you via SMS / Email to confirm your spot and send through the payment details (PayID or Bank Transfer).
